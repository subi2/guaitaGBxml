User guide

Guide to the guaitaGBXML v0.13 app: what it shows, how to navigate it and how to read the results.

Open the appHome page

01

What it is

guaitaGBXML is a gbXML file viewer and analyser. It shows the model geometry in 3D and the construction data (spaces, surfaces, openings, constructions, materials and zones), and lets you review the envelope and validate the data before passing the model to simulation.

Geometry is read-only: the app does not move or modify any vertex. Editing affects the data only.

02

Getting started
  • Open a .xml or .gbxml file with the open button, or drag it into the window.
  • The model loads and the view fits automatically.
  • Click an element to see its data.

03

View and navigation
  • Orthogonal and perspective views, with the view cube to orient yourself and switch quickly between faces.
  • The navigation panel (on the left) holds the tools menu.
  • You can split the screen into several views and choose a light or dark background.

04

Data and editing

You can correct names, types and assigned constructions. Every change can be undone. The original file is not modified: changes are saved to a new file.

05

Sections and cutting planes

The dual section slider controls Z (vertical, with storey marks), X and Y, and the rotation of the cutting plane. Cutting planes are shown with a box outline so you can see where the cut is.

06

Envelope analysis
  • Areas by surface and opening type.
  • U-value calculated from the layers: R = thickness / λ, plus surface resistances. If the declared U does not match the calculated one, the app reports it.
  • Window-to-wall ratio (WWR), compactness and K coefficient.
  • Comparison with the CTE DB-HE1 limits by climate zone (α to E).

Results are indicative: they do not replace a formal calculation report.

07

Adjacencies and sun

The adjacency matrix shows which surfaces share a space. The sun position is calculated by date and time, and shadows are projected onto the model.

08

Validation

Validation reports more than twenty warning codes: surfaces without a space, openings outside their surface, constructions without layers, text encoding errors and other inconsistencies. Each warning names the affected element so you can find it in the model.

09

Comparing models

You can compare two gbXML files from the same project. The app shows the differences element by element (added, removed and changed), useful for reviewing how a model evolves.

10

Export

Results and tables can be exported as CSV and XLS. Geometry can be exported as OBJ, MTL and glTF for other programs.

11

Large files

Models with tens of thousands of surfaces are processed in a background worker, so the interface does not freeze. Tables are virtual: only the visible rows are drawn.

12

Offline use

The app can be installed as an application on your phone or computer from the browser. After the first load, it works without a connection.

13

Limitations
  • It is not an official certification or CTE compliance tool.
  • A gbXML file may contain encoding errors (for example, UTF-8 names read as double-encoded text) or incomplete data. The app flags them, but final verification is the user's responsibility.
  • Compare results with the original model before making design decisions.

14

Frequently asked questions
Why can't I move the geometry?

Because geometry is read-only. Only the data attached to each element is edited.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. All processing happens in the browser. The app does not send any file or data to a server.
